Stock PS3 XMB pictograms decoded from this user's locally installed firmware:
dev_flash/vsh/resource/qgl/icontex.qrc

These are the original 128x128 BGRA normal maps with their original silhouettes,
rendered with neutral silver illumination. They are not redrawn vectors and do
not use the low-resolution 46px RCO fallback images or the gold P3T theme.

Output: 256x256 RGBA PNG. Each glyph's nontransparent bounds are centered with
the longest visible dimension normalized to 192px, retaining its aspect ratio.
The DDS rows are vertically flipped during export because the firmware texture
storage is bottom-to-top; this keeps the briefcase, controller, note, and other
symbols upright in the launcher. The normalization is intended for 96px or
smaller UI boxes; the 256px canvas does not imply newly invented texture detail.
No blur or glow is applied.

Reproduce with scripts/extract-qrc-icons.cjs <local icontex.qrc> assets/icons-hd --assets
dimensions.json records every original resource ID and source visible bounds.

These Sony firmware-derived assets are for this local personal-use build.
