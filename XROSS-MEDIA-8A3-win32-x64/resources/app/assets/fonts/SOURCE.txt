The three SCE-PS3-RD Latin fonts were copied from the user's locally installed
RPCS3 dev_flash/data/font directory for this personal launcher on 2026-09-23.
They are Sony/FONTWORKS firmware assets and are not covered by XROSS-MEDIA-8A3's MIT
source-code license. The CSS family alias is "PS3 Rodin".

The PSP emulator firmware contains PGF bitmap fonts, not a browser-compatible
outline font. The launcher uses the real PS3 Rodin fonts for both XMB styles;
it does not claim that a substituted desktop font is the exact PSP PGF font.
